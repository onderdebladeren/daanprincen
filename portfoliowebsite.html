Daan Princen

I am a wanderer, a city nomad. Who works with whatever it bumps a foot against. Without a fixed medium, rather a crater of leftover materials that keeps spewing stuff until there is a revelation there. A revelation that is lingering in the mezzanine of fact and fiction. Rich in representation, empty in matter. Within that same emptiness, it finds the right to exist. With urge to slip out of functionality.

It always starts with a walk. This is how I am enchanted by the versatility of public space. The deep urge to walk, I pause and take time at situations and objects that would fleetingly pass me by with a vehicle. On foot as an authentic form. It is a constant journey of discovery through a seemingly 'familiar' landscape. The fact that all possible psychogeographical variations can occur in the city is a wondrous thing. Thoughts and ideas become tangible entities at leisure.

Daagnabbit Collective

Long term collaboration with Guus Harbers. Started out when we were living together and sharing a studio, we began handing over old drawings, texts and collages to each other to remix them and create new works. Resulting in mainly zines and experimental publications. Occasionally meeting at a risoprinter to create scattered graphics out of leftover paper and doodles. Making sure together that nothing we create goes unnoticed or is going to waste.

Late 2024 we've participated in SPREAD Zine Fest at het resort in Groningen. And in February 2026 at the Xtra Terrestrial Zine Fair at Dokhuis in Rotterdam.
